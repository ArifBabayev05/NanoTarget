Three layers, one decision at the endpoint

01

Attach time

Agent tools leave traces the instant they take control: overlay markers, injected globals, evaluated-script read bursts, focus emulation, signed requests. Measured 0.1–0.5 s after attach — before the agent’s first action.

02

Pointer physics

A hand’s path is curved, its tremor grows with speed and comes in bursts, it slows onto the target and holds 83–225 ms. Drivers teleport and release in 1–4 ms; Bézier bots are parabola-clean. A boosted-tree model, cross-validated across people and devices: AUC 0.999, zero human false positives.

03

Seal & reclaim

Data already on screen is redacted in the browser the moment an indicator appears — the agent’s read sees ••••. Once attached, the session stays “agent” until the person proves presence with a passkey (Touch ID) and takes it back.
